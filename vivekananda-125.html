Dr Nikhil Yadav to lead 'Vivekananda 125' initiative connecting 50 campuses and 75 villages

A youth-focused initiative to run from 11 September 2026 to 12 January 2028, bringing Swami Vivekananda's ideas into dialogue with young people and communities across India.
📅 11 September 2026 — 12 January 2028

A youth-oriented initiative titled "VIVEKANANDA 125 — From Campus to Community" will be undertaken from 11 September 2026 to 12 January 2028, marking the 125th anniversary of Swami Vivekananda's Mahasamadhi (Punyatithi). The initiative aims to take conversations around Swami Vivekananda beyond conventional commemorative programmes and connect his ideas with contemporary questions facing young Indians.
Campus 50
Under Campus 50, students will be engaged through lectures, interactive discussions, reading activities, creative outreach and digital initiatives. Conversations will focus on themes such as "Know Thyself," strength, character, leadership, purpose and responsible use of technology.
Community 75
Under Community 75, the initiative will engage with village youth, students, teachers and community leaders through interactions and Youth Chaupals. The programme will also document inspiring local personalities, village stories, traditions and community achievements.
Digital Community — Swami Vivekananda for Gen Z
A digital community, "Swami Vivekananda for Gen Z," will provide continued engagement through short videos, writings, reading recommendations, discussions and simple youth challenges focused on areas such as digital discipline and Surya Namaskar.
"The objective is not simply to tell young people about Swami Vivekananda, but to create opportunities for them to question, reflect, discover and contribute. We want to explore how Swamiji's ideas of strength, character, self-belief and service can become meaningful in the lives of young Indians today." — Dr Nikhil Yadav
Led by Dr Nikhil Yadav — PhD from Jawaharlal Nehru University; Founder, Swami Vivekananda for GenZ; Deputy Head, Vivekananda Kendra Uttar Prant.

LEAD: India and Belgium formalised their most consequential defence-industrial engagement to date on Wednesday, 3 September 2026, on the second day of Belgian Prime Minister Bart De Wever's first official visit to India. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and his Belgian counterpart Theo Francken signed a Letter of Intent providing the framework for structural cooperation between the Indian Armed Forces and the Belgian Armed Forces. Ten industrial agreements between Belgian defence companies and Indian partners were signed alongside the ministerial instrument. Fifteen Belgian defence companies, most represented by their CEOs, joined the delegation.

## The Letter of Intent

The government-to-government instrument covers five dimensions of cooperation: training and capacity building, officer exchanges, joint R&D, seminars and joint exercises, and maritime security cooperation. Both sides also committed to developing an industrial roadmap for identifying future projects. The Belgian Defence College plans to organise its World Study Trip in India in 2027. Francken framed the shift the Letter of Intent represents as moving from trust-building to concrete delivery — joint training, industrial projects, and company opportunities rather than non-binding declarations.

## The BSDI-SIDM umbrella agreement

Alongside the ministerial Letter of Intent, the Belgian Security & Defence Industry (BSDI) and the Society of Indian Defence Manufacturers (SIDM) signed a cooperation agreement between the two countries' defence-industry sector organisations. The framework opens the operational entry point for Belgian firms to participate in co-development under Make in India — the substantive institutional bridge through which the industrial partnerships announced during the visit will progressively be implemented.

## The ten industrial partnerships

Ammunition, rockets and weapons. The Liège-based New Lachaussée will supply Tembo Classic Engineering with a complete production line for two ammunition calibres, plus tooling for two additional calibres — approximately 100 million rounds annual capacity, €50 million contract value, delivery in 2028 and 2029. Thales Belgium and Kalyani Strategic Systems will bring 70mm rocket assembly to India, with the first entirely Indian-made rocket expected in early 2027. FN Herstal and Kalyani Strategic Systems are exploring the establishment of a joint production centre for FN weapons, integrated weapon systems, and counter-drone systems.

Maritime security. The largest project of the mission is between Exail Belgium and Larsen & Toubro — Exail will supply the autonomous toolboxes used to detect, identify and neutralise naval mines on twelve mine countermeasure vessels being built by L&T, with the programme potentially expanding to as many as 59. Anglo Belgian Corporation, based in Ghent, is in discussions with Kirloskar Oil Engines on marine propulsion — Kirloskar is building India's first fully indigenous 6 MW marine diesel engine, and ABC's expertise could accelerate delivery.

Armoured platforms. John Cockerill Defense is a candidate to supply the turret for the Zorawar, India's light tank being developed for high-altitude operations along the border with China. The bid received political-level support during the March 2026 Princess Astrid economic mission; the next stage is operational implementation. OIP Sensor Systems is continuing cooperation with India Optel Limited on electro-optical systems for the Arjun main battle tank — OIP has supplied over 130 sights for Arjun MK I, and has developed a new fire-control system for Arjun MK II capable of launching laser-guided missiles.

Air defence, drones and infrastructure protection. Octave, the Leuven-based visualisation software specialist, continues its cooperation with Bharat Electronics Limited on air-defence systems including Akash Teer, ARUDHRA, IPSS and IACCS. Flemish drone manufacturer Sol.One is working with DGVK Enterprises to introduce the Saboteur drone and the Buzzkill and Hitchhiker interceptors to the Indian market, with Indian Armed Forces testing pending. Pitagone, the Brussels-based manufacturer of mobile vehicle-attack protection barriers, is establishing Pitagone India as a joint venture with Krishna Allied — Pitagone Belgium retains a 51 per cent majority stake and intends to use the Indian operation as a base for Southeast Asian markets.

## Why the alignment works

Belgian defence-industrial strengths sit close to Indian priorities. Belgium is historically a global leader in mine countermeasure technology, and holds substantive expertise across ammunition manufacturing, electro-optical sensors, marine engines, air-defence software architecture, and light drones. India progressively requires capability across each of these categories — some for domestic modernisation, some for export competitiveness through Make in India localisation. The Zorawar turret bid and the mine countermeasure programme carry particular operational weight given the maritime and land-border security environments India navigates.

## The wider frame

Francken articulated the strategic logic behind Belgium's engagement in terms that capture the current European defence-doctrinal shift. Russia's war in Ukraine, its military cooperation with Iran and North Korea, and Chinese assertiveness in the Indo-Pacific together mean that European and Indo-Pacific security have become interconnected dimensions of a single environment. For an open trading nation like Belgium, whose economic architecture depends on sea-lane security, working closely with strong Indo-Pacific partners like India has become strategically essential — militarily, technologically, and industrially. The recent appointment of a Belgian Defence Attaché in New Delhi was the institutional signal; the current defence-industrial package is the operational delivery.

De Wever will travel to Mumbai on Thursday, 4 September, for business engagements in the diamond, ports and logistics sectors, before concluding the visit. What has been signed in New Delhi across the 3 September engagements — the Letter of Intent, the BSDI-SIDM umbrella, and the ten industrial agreements — together constitutes one of the most significant defence-industrial packages any European partner has activated with India in recent years.
